How the scanner works

  1. Night scan

    Confirm the daily trend using swing structure and two consecutive closes through the reference wick. Shortlist valid Phase 1 trends after checking for a breach of the protected swing.

  2. Morning scan

    Recheck the daily trend and look for the same direction on the 4-hour and 1-hour charts.

  3. EMA check

    Check the 20, 50 and 200 EMA stack. A completed H1 candle that touches the 20 EMA and opens and closes on the trend side qualifies for manual review.

The scanner runs locally through MetaTrader 5. This page shows a saved report.
